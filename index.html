Showdist Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 September 2026

Showdist ("the app") is developed by Carlos Cruz. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information the app uses

The app uses your device's camera to show the augmented-reality view and to measure distances, and its motion sensors for the level. Camera images and sensor readings are processed on your device in real time. The app does not record, save or transmit photos, video or sensor data.

Measurements you save

Measurements you choose to save (their points, lengths, date and unit) are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded by the app. They are deleted when you delete them in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall it. If you export a PDF or share a measurement as text, it goes only to the app or person you choose in Android's share sheet.

No accounts, ads or analytics

The app has no sign-in, shows no ads, and contains no analytics or tracking code. The developer does not collect, sell or share any personal information.

Google Play Services for AR

This application runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google LLC and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
